# Product features

MOVA is designed to help freelancers and small teams keep delivery and financial work connected in one place.

## Dashboard

- Overview of project progress and current work.
- Upcoming tasks and events.
- Financial totals and workspace notes.

## Client management

- Client profiles and lifecycle states.
- Association between clients and their projects.
- Search and status-oriented views.

## Project management

- Project records and ownership.
- Lifecycle states: planned, in progress, completed, paused, and cancelled.
- Project-linked tasks, milestones, and financial entries.

## Task management

- Work organized into Kanban-friendly states.
- Priorities, assignments, due dates, and milestones.
- Connections to projects and relevant partners.

## Calendar

- Scheduled and cancelled events.
- Meetings, deadlines, and reminders.
- Workspace time-zone handling.

## Partners and collaboration

- External partner records.
- Participation in projects, including active and ended relationships.

## Financial management

- Accounts receivable and payable.
- Partial settlements and remaining balances.
- Payment reversals and financial history.
- Cancellation-related rules to preserve consistent records.

## Demo boundaries

The current product is a private **demonstration build** with synthetic data. Public self-registration and unrestricted demo access are not enabled. Features listed here describe the implemented prototype and should not be read as production service commitments.

# Product status and roadmap

This roadmap communicates directions for the MOVA product. It is not a commitment to specific delivery dates.

## Implemented in the private demo

- Workspace dashboard.
- Client and project records.
- Tasks, status workflows, assignments, and milestones.
- Partner records and project participation.
- Calendar events and time-zone handling.
- Receivables, payables, settlement movements, and reversals.
- Authentication and database access controls.
- Docker-based deployment and a protected demo environment.

## Next product iterations

- More streamlined onboarding and workspace creation.
- Expanded workspace membership administration.
- Richer financial reporting and exports.
- More flexible filters, search, and productivity workflows.
- Accessibility, responsiveness, and performance refinements.
- Production operations, account recovery, monitoring, and deployment hardening.

## Product stage

**Private demo / evolving prototype.** The scope above is not a statement that MOVA is publicly available as a commercial SaaS.
